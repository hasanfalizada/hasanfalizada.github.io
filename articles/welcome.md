# Welcome to My Technical Blog

Hello and welcome to my technical blog! I'm Hasan Alizada, an Enterprise Architect with extensive experience in building scalable and fault-tolerant systems. This blog is where I share my insights, experiences, and thoughts on various aspects of enterprise architecture, system design, and modern development practices.

## What You Can Expect

In this blog, I'll be covering a wide range of topics that are close to my heart and expertise:

### Enterprise Architecture
- **TOGAF Framework**: Best practices, implementation strategies, and real-world applications
- **System Design**: Scalable architectures, microservices patterns, and distributed systems
- **Digital Transformation**: Aligning technology with business goals and driving organizational change

### Technical Deep Dives
- **Java Ecosystem**: Spring Boot, microservices, and modern Java development
- **Cloud Architecture**: Oracle Cloud, AWS, and cloud-native solutions
- **DevOps Practices**: CI/CD pipelines, containerization with Docker and Kubernetes

### Leadership and Management
- **Team Leadership**: Building and mentoring high-performing development teams
- **Project Management**: Agile methodologies, risk management, and delivery excellence
- **Technology Strategy**: IT governance, budgeting, and strategic decision-making

## My Background

With over 15 years of experience in the IT industry, I've had the privilege of working with some of Azerbaijan's leading organizations, including Azerbaijan Railways and Kapital Bank. I hold multiple professional certifications including TOGAF® Enterprise Architecture Practitioner, PMP®, ITIL®, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Certified Architect Professional.

My journey has taken me through various roles - from hands-on development to system architecture and enterprise-level strategic planning. This diverse experience gives me a unique perspective on the challenges and opportunities in modern IT landscapes.
